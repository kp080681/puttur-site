Dr. Sachin M. Shetty – Consultant Dermatologist, Puttur

Dr. Sachin M. Shetty is a Consultant Dermatologist and Aesthetic Physician with over 12 years of experience in clinical dermatology, cosmetology, and aesthetic medicine. He holds an MBBS and MD in Dermatology and is an International Fellow of the American Academy of Dermatology (IFAAD), as well as an International Junior Fellow of the European Academy of Dermatology and Venereology (EADV).
Trained under some of India’s most respected dermatologists, Dr. Shetty combines a strong academic foundation with extensive hands-on clinical experience. He regularly participates in national and international dermatology conferences and workshops to stay updated with the latest evidence-based advances, bringing modern and ethical dermatologic care to his hometown of Puttur.

Read More

His clinical practice encompasses the management of psoriasis, acne and acne scarring, hair loss disorders, and LASER-based dermatological procedures. The clinic is equipped with advanced diagnostic facilities, including dermoscopy and trichoscopy, along with state-of-the-art LASER systems, precision dermatosurgical equipment, medical-grade chemical peels, and injectable therapies sourced from internationally accredited manufacturers. These facilities enable accurate diagnosis and the delivery of standardised, evidence-based, and individualised patient care.
Dr. Shetty’s vision is to make safe, ethical, and personalised dermatological care accessible to the people of Puttur and the surrounding regions, guided by a strong commitment to patient well-being and trust. In addition to his clinical practice, he is actively involved in community service, regularly conducting free dermatology outreach programmes in rural areas and organising multiple annual free consultation camps at his clinic for economically disadvantaged individuals and those with limited access to specialised dermatological care.
He is deeply committed to helping patients achieve and maintain healthy skin and hair through transparent, evidence-based, and individualised care. With a strong emphasis on safety and long-term well-being, he follows a careful, stepwise approach to treatment—avoiding unnecessary medications and prioritising lifestyle guidance, dietary modifications, and safer treatment options wherever appropriate.

Our Services

Q-Switched Nd: YAG LASER

The Q-Switched Nd:YAG LASER is a state-of-the-art, non-invasive treatment option used to address a variety of cosmetic concerns. It utilizes high-energy light pulses to target and break down unwanted pigment in the skin. This technology is highly effective in treating pigmentation issues, scars, skin rejuvenation, and tattoos, offering a safe and efficient solution for patients looking to improve their skin’s appearance.

Triple Wavelength Diode Laser

Our Triple Wavelength Diode Laser treatment is designed to make your skin smooth, bright, and beautiful. It reduces unwanted hair, fades dark spots, and refreshes your skin—all in one go! This gentle and safe treatment is tailored just for you, leaving you feeling comfortable and looking radiant.

308nm Excimer LASER

Our 308nm Excimer Laser is a breakthrough treatment that helps with skin conditions like vitiligo, psoriasis, and atopic dermatitis. Using gentle ultraviolet light, it targets damaged skin and boosts healthy renewal and repigmentation. This safe, non-invasive therapy is designed to give you noticeable results with minimal discomfort or side effects. Let your skin feel healthy and refreshed again!

Radiofrequency Ablation

Get rid of unwanted growths and more with Radiofrequency Ablation. This gentle treatment uses heat energy to target problem areas, offering relief with minimal discomfort. It’s quick, safe, and allows you to get back to your daily routine in no time.

Chemical Peeling

Say goodbye to dull skin! Our chemical peel treatment removes dead skin layers to uncover smooth, radiant skin. It reduces wrinkles, acne scars, and dark spots, leaving your skin fresh and vibrant. With options tailored to your skin type, this non-invasive treatment is your key to a flawless look.

Hair Treatment

Our trichology treatments are designed to tackle hair loss, dandruff, and scalp issues. With our comprehensive approach, we’ll work with you to create a customized care plan that boosts hair growth, strengthens follicles, and improves scalp health. Bring back the confidence that comes with great hair!

Anti-aging Treatments

We offer advanced anti-aging treatments to help restore a more youthful and radiant appearance. Our treatments include dermal fillers to restore lost volume, and laser resurfacing to promote collagen production and improve skin texture. By combining these cutting-edge technologies and personalized care, we help our patients achieve a refreshed and revitalized look.

Other Treatments for Various Skin Disorders

We provide advanced treatments for skin and hair concerns, including Cryotherapy for warts and lesions, Dermatosurgery for scars and growths, and FUE Hair Transplantation for natural hair restoration. Our Vitiligo surgeries, like skin grafting and melanocyte transplantation, restore pigmentation and confidence.

What is the cost of LASER Treatment?

The cost of LASER treatment varies depending on several factors, including the type and severity of your skin concern, the area to be treated, and the duration of the condition.

To get an accurate estimate, we recommend consulting our expert doctor for a personalized evaluation. This will enable us to provide you with a tailored treatment plan and a clear understanding of the costs involved.

Do you remove moles on the face?

Yes, we do. Our treatment approach varies depending on the size, location, and depth of the mole. For superficial moles, we use advanced techniques such as chemical cautery, Q-switched Nd:YAG laser, and radiofrequency ablation. For deeper moles, we may recommend electrocautery or elliptical excision.

Our expert Doctor will assess your mole and create a personalized treatment plan to achieve optimal results with minimal to no scars.
